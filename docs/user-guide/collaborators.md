# Collaborators and roles

Everyone who works on a site should have **their own account** — never share one password among a group. Accounts are network-wide, so a person can be an author on your site and the administrator of their own.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Everything on this site: settings, theme, members, plugins offered by the platform, import/export, deletion |
| **Editor** | Publish and manage all posts and pages, moderate comments, manage categories |
| **Author** | Publish and manage their own posts, upload media |
| **Contributor** | Write and edit their own drafts; an editor publishes them |
| **Subscriber** | Read (including members-only sites) and manage their own profile |

No site role — not even Administrator — can install software, edit code, use unfiltered HTML or affect other sites.

## Adding people

Go to **Users → Add New**:

* **Add Existing User** — enter the email of someone who already has an account on the platform and choose a role. They receive an email to confirm joining.
* **Add User** — create a new account; the person receives an email to set their own password.

Depending on the platform's policy, some email domains may be refused for everyone.

## Changing or removing access

On **Users**, change a member's role or remove them from the site. Removing someone from your site does not delete their account or their other sites; their posts stay and you can reassign them.

Role changes and membership changes are recorded in the platform's security event log (without any content).
